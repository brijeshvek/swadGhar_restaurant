# 🔐 SwadGhar Restaurant - System Credentials & Franchise Staff Structure

This document contains all login credentials for **Central Admin**, **5 Franchise Branch Managers**, and sample customer accounts.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Staff Access Policy**: In the SwadGhar system, **only the General Branch Manager of each franchise branch has a system login ID & password**.
> All other staff members (Chefs, Masters, Captains, Cashiers, Stewards) are registered as operational team members under their respective franchise.
> - **Branch Manager**: Can log in to view and manage **ONLY their own branch's staff team, roster, and live orders**.
> - **Central Admin**: Can log in to view **ALL 5 franchises, change manager login credentials (ID & password), and manage staff across all locations**.
